Scientific calculator

−2^2 = −4 and (−2)^2 = 4. ×10ˣ multiplies by a power of ten; use xʸ for powers. Exact results at 0°, 90° and other cardinal angles; tan(90°) reports Undefined. Factorials run 0 to 170.

BMI calculator

Enter height and weight.

For information and screening only. This is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. BMI can’t tell muscle from fat or show where fat sits, and it isn’t suited to children, pregnancy, or eating-disorder assessment. WHO’s 2004 review kept 25 and 30 as global cutoffs and treated 23.0, 27.5, 32.5 and 37.5 as public-health action points for Asian populations. Pair BMI with waist size: risk rises from 90 cm for South Asian, Chinese and Japanese men, 94 cm for white European men, and 80 cm for women. Never use this site in a medical emergency. Height and weight stay in your browser.

GNI calculator

GNI here means Gross National Income, a country-level measure. Looking for pay after tax? That’s a gross-to-net salary tool. Looking for diabetes GMI? Use the small calculator below.

Figures are national averages. They don’t show inequality, household affordability or informal activity, and Atlas dollars differ from purchasing power. Countries with several exchange-rate systems need weighted rates, and persistent high inflation limits Atlas accuracy. Classification output uses World Bank FY2027 thresholds (1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, based on 2025 Atlas GNI per capita).

Glucose Management Indicator (GMI)
GMI % = 3.31 + 0.02392 × mean glucose. Needs 12 or more days of CGM data. Informational only, not medical advice.
